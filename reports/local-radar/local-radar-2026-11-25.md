## BEST ARTICLE LEADS

**#1 ARTICLE LEAD: the SNWA nonfunctional-grass timeline** (READY)
- **Reader question:** does the Jan 1 grass ban affect my HOA, my apartment complex or my yard, and when do fines actually start?
- **Confirmed in SNWA documents (read):**
  - **The law:**
    - AB 356 (2021) bans using Colorado River water on nonfunctional turf.
    - AB 220 (2023) exempted properties used exclusively as a single-family residence.
    - The ban starts Jan 1, 2027.
  - **Who it applies to:** commercial, multifamily, government and other properties, but not single-family front and back yards (SNWA laws page).
  - **Fees:** the resolution supports water-waste charges only after Jan 1, 2028. Owners who sign an abatement agreement with their water provider have 2028 charges waived.
  - **Waiver timeline:**
    - Apply by Apr 1, 2027.
    - SNWA decides by Jul 1, 2027.
    - Appeals are due by Sep 1, 2027.
    - Appeals are decided by Dec 1, 2027.
    - By Jan 1, 2028, SNWA sends member agencies the list of properties with remaining nonfunctional turf.
  - **Streetscape strips:** waivers for these strips on single-family parcels continue. This doesn't change HOA obligations.
  - **Rebates:** kept through Jan 1, 2029. The amount is $5/sq ft, per Fox5.
- **From Fox5 (read):**
  - Enforcement runs through the water-waste processes of LVVWD, Henderson, North Las Vegas and Boulder City. A warning comes first.
  - About 19M sq ft of nonfunctional grass remains. Replacing it would save more than 1B gallons a year.
- **Verification concerns:**
  - **The vote:** the official vote record is pending in SNWA minutes. Approval is confirmed only by Fox5 reporting.
  - **2028 fine amounts:** set by each member agency. LVVWD's September amounts ($80–$5,120) were only a proposal.
  - **HOA/resident lawsuit:** status unknown, from an RJ headline only.
  - **Local rules:** whether LVVWD, Henderson and NLV have adopted their service-rule changes.
- **Must not claim:**
  - that the ban itself is delayed
  - that single-family yards are affected

  - any outcome of the lawsuit
