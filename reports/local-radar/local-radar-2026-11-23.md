**#1 ARTICLE LEAD: Opportunity Village Northwest Campus** (READY)
- **Reader question:** what's being built at Rome and Thom by the Costco at Decatur and the 215?
- **Confirmed (Opportunity Village sources):**
  - **The project:** $59M on 17.6 acres. It's the nonprofit's fourth large campus.
  - **Campus:** at 5080 W. Rome Blvd, with a 16,500 sq ft program-services building for day habilitation and prevocational programs.
  - **Public-facing space:** about 17,000 sq ft at 5030 W. Rome Blvd for the OVintage Boutique, a cafe and the OV Artworks studio and gallery.
  - **Betty's Village North:** at 6700 Thom Blvd, with 90 residences and 126 bedrooms (1-BR 730 sq ft, 2-BR 1,018 sq ft, 4-BR 2,050 sq ft). It has a clubhouse of about 12,000 sq ft with a pool, hot tub and accessible gym, plus paths, a barbecue area and a community park.
  - **Who lives there:** adults with and without disabilities, with on-site concierge staff and 24-hour support available.
  - **Demand:** the first Betty's Village (2021) filled within five months, and there's a waiting list of hundreds.
- **Concerns:**
  - The first-resident month and move-in period differ across OV's own documents.
  - No opening or ribbon cutting has been announced.
