## TOP 5 TONIGHT

1. **THIRD Street arts venue at the former Downtown Cinemas** (814 S. Third St, Arts District). **BOTH.**
   - **Upgraded from CONDITIONAL to READY.** I read the city's Redevelopment Agency (RDA) agenda summary for Nov 4, 2026 and the draft reimbursement agreement.
   - **The deal:** the city repays up to $4.2M in eligible construction costs, from the RDA Special Revenue Fund. The repayment depends on THIRD Street raising its share, about $3.3M.
   - **Phase 1A:** the old Cinemas 1 and 2 become one flexible studio theatre of about 250 seats, plus a lobby, a new stair, a concession stand and backstage space. The rough budget is $7,470,126.
   - **Timeline:**
     - The agreement targets a construction start within 9 months of its effective date, which isn't known yet. The latest allowed start is 12 months.
     - Substantial completion is targeted 8 months after construction starts.
     - Fox5 projects an opening around summer 2028 (tentative).
   - **Why LVINIT should care:** a closed downtown landmark gets a new use as an arts anchor. LVINIT has no coverage.
   - **Primary source: READ AND VERIFIED** (agenda and draft agreement). The vote outcome comes from Fox5.
   - **Next:** this is tomorrow's assignment.
