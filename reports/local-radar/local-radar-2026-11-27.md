## TOMORROW'S RECOMMENDED LVINIT ASSIGNMENT

**Status: READY — FIRST-PARTY MEDIA RECOMMENDED**

- **Working headline:** The Henderson DMV is gone: what to know about Nevada's new Silverado DMV in the south valley
- **Location:** 9850 S. Valley View Blvd, Las Vegas 89141, just south of W. Silverado Ranch Blvd (south valley).
- **Why this is tomorrow's best story:**
  - Every newcomer has to visit the DMV, and the valley's DMV map changed this fall.
  - Henderson no longer has a DMV office, and CDL operations moved from North Las Vegas to the south valley.
  - It has durable search value and links naturally from `/guides/moving-to-las-vegas`.
  - Everything is primary-verified tonight.
  - LVINIT has no DMV office coverage.
- **What happened:**
  - Nevada DMV merged its Henderson office and its Donovan Way CDL office into one new Silverado facility.
  - It opened Mon Oct 26, and the ribbon cutting was Nov 12.
- **Confirmed facts:**
  - **From the DMV release of Oct 12 (read):**
    - Opened to the public Mon Oct 26, 2026.
    - 72,619 sq ft. It houses the main DMV office and CDL operations.
    - The director calls it "the largest DMV facility in Nevada."
    - It replaces the 18,540 sq ft Henderson office, which was built in 1997.
    - The main building has 60 counters, two drive-through kiosks, four after-hours walk-up kiosks and 301 customer parking spaces.
    - The CDL building has 15 counters, 5 Motor Carrier counters, a full-size CDL testing course, 42 parking spaces and 16 semi-truck spaces.
  - **From the DMV release of Sept 8 (read):**
    - The Donovan Way CDL office closed Oct 9.
    - The Henderson office on American Pacific Dr closed Oct 14.
    - Both closures are permanent.
    - DMV asks people to do renewals, address changes and driver-history requests online through MyDMV or DriveNV.
  - **From the DMV release of July 16 (read):**
    - The director says funding constraints drove the decision.
    - AAA's offices on W. Sunset Rd and N. Green Valley Pkwy in Henderson handle registration, moving permits and duplicate titles.
  - **From the DMV office locations page (read tonight):**
    - Hours are 8 a.m.–5 p.m. weekdays and 8 a.m.–4 p.m. Saturdays.
    - There's a VIN station.
    - The page says **"Appointment Required!"**
    - It lists no Henderson DMV office.
- **Primary sources read and verified:**
  - [DMV release: Silverado grand opening](https://dmv.nv.gov/news/26012_Silverado_grand_opening.htm)
  - [DMV release: Henderson and Donovan consolidation](https://dmv.nv.gov/news/26010_Henderson_and_Donovan_DMV_offices_to_consolidate_into_silverado_facility.htm)
  - [DMV release: Silverado update, July 16](https://dmv.nv.gov/news/26009_Nevada_DMV_provides_updates_on_Silverado_Ranch.htm)
