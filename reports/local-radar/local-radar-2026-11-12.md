## TOP 5 TONIGHT

1. **Nevada State Veterans Memorial, new home** (Thunderbird Family Sports Complex, 6105 N. Durango Dr, northwest Las Vegas). **BOTH.**
   - **What happened:** the city scheduled a free public unveiling for Veterans Day, Wed Nov 11, starting 4:30 p.m.
   - **What's there:** 18 bronze statues by Douwe Blumberg, each about 7.5 ft tall. They came from outside the former Grant Sawyer state building and now stand in a park setting with a promenade, an amphitheater and signs.
   - **Funding:** the council accepted $3M from the state on Nov 4 (Fox5).
   - **Primary source: READ AND VERIFIED.** The City of Las Vegas release confirms the scheduled opening and the details above.
   - **Not yet confirmed:** that the ceremony actually happened as scheduled. I found no post-event report.
   - **Why LVINIT should care:** it's a brand-new, very visual public place in the northwest, free to visit, and LVINIT has no coverage.
   - **Next:** this is tomorrow's assignment, with a morning photo visit.
2. **Blue Diamond Wash Trail, Durango Dr to Hualapai Way** (southwest valley / Enterprise). **WRITE.**
   - **What happened:** ceremonial groundbreaking on Tue Nov 10 with Commissioner Justin Jones (KSNV).
   - **Primary source: READ AND VERIFIED.** The Clark County construction notice gives:
     - a paved, ADA-compliant trail 12 to 15 ft wide
     - an at-grade crossing at Fort Apache Rd
     - connections to existing trailheads at each end
     - contractor Unicon LLC, contract $2,790,000
     - construction start Aug 31, 2026; estimated completion February 2027 (county best estimate)
   - **Why LVINIT should care:** a new walk and bike route through fast-growing southwest neighborhoods. LVINIT has no coverage.
   - **Next:** Publisher Fallback #1.
