## BEST ARTICLE LEADS

**#1 ARTICLE LEAD: CCSD facility plan** (READY)
- **Reader question:** is my kid's school, or the school near the house I'm looking at, closing or being rebuilt?
- **Confirmed by CCSD:**
  - **2027-28:**
    - Toland International Academy's magnet program moves to the new Matt Kelly campus, which includes a community center.
    - Rundle Elementary students move to Keller Elementary, which gets an extensive renovation by 2028-29. Students west of Nellis go to Edwards; students south of Charleston go to Mendoza.
  - **2028-29:**
    - Kahre Elementary consolidates with Eisenberg Elementary, and the Kahre campus becomes the CCSD School Police HQ.
    - The renovated Molasky Pre-K–8 Magnet opens in fall 2028.
    - Leavitt Middle School gets a renovation.
  - **2029-30:**
    - McCall and Cahlan students mostly move to the rebuilt Bridger Pre-K–8 Magnet (2029). Some go to Lincoln, or to Herron, which is being reconstructed by 2030.
    - Sewell Elementary consolidates into the rebuilt Brown Pre-K–8 Magnet (fall 2029). Mannion Middle School gets a renovation.
  - **Process:** 55 forums and 7,962 survey responses.
