## BEST ARTICLE LEADS

**#1 ARTICLE LEAD: Red Rock Legacy Trail. The first mile is open.**
- **Reader question:** can I ride or walk toward Red Rock from Summerlin yet, where do I park, and when does the rest get built?
- **Confirmed (county page, read):**
  - The full trail is about 19 miles, 12 feet wide, from the Summerlin edge to Blue Diamond Rd.
  - Phase 1 runs 0.99 mi from Charleston/Sky Vista to the relocated Red Rock sign. Construction began 7/14/25.
  - **Phase 1 also added:**
    - a signal and trailhead lot at Charleston/Sky Vista
    - deceleration lanes
  - **The Phase 1 extension** to the Visitor Center and Scenic Loop was funded by RTC in Dec 2024. It has no start date.
  - **Later phases:**
    - Phase 2: 6.61 mi, design 70% as of Aug 2025
    - Phases 3 and 4: 3.5 mi and 2.1 mi
    - Phase 5: about 3.9 mi to a future Hualapai/Blue Diamond trailhead
  - **Funding:** FLAP and SNPLMA grants.
  - **Opening:** Apr 28, 2026, per KSNV and Fox5.
- **Concerns:**
  - The county page hasn't been updated for the opening, so attribute the opening date to news reports.
  - Don't give a total cost. The ~$100M figure comes from older trade press.
  - The phase lengths add up to about 17.1 mi. Say "about 19 miles, per Clark County."
  - Don't state whether the BLM Scenic Loop is open or closed. Fox5's Nov 12–13 story contradicts itself, and the loop is a different facility anyway.
