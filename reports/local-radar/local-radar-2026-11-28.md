## TOMORROW'S RECOMMENDED LVINIT ASSIGNMENT

**Status: READY — FIRST-PARTY MEDIA RECOMMENDED**

- **Working headline:** The 215 trail now goes under Charleston: what changed for Summerlin walkers and riders
- **Location:** the CC-215 / W. Charleston Blvd interchange in Summerlin. The ribbon cutting was at the NW corner of W. Charleston and the beltway. The project also built a trail extension west of the 215 between Fort Apache Rd and Peace Way.
- **Why this is tomorrow's best story:**
  - It removes one of the valley's worst trail crossings: eight lanes of Charleston at grade.
  - It fits LVINIT's trail coverage: Summerlin Parkway Trail, Red Rock Legacy Trail, Northern Beltway Trail and Blue Diamond Wash Trail.
  - LVINIT has no coverage of it. I checked `main` tonight.
  - It's the only lead with a verified scope and an opening confirmed by more than one source.
- **What happened:**
  - Clark County finished an RTC-funded safety project on the CC-215 Western Beltway Trail, reported at about $18.7M.
  - It built tunnels under Charleston Blvd, so trail users no longer cross the road.
  - It also built a 1,635-ft segment that links the trail south of Peace Way to the segment north of Fort Apache Rd.
  - Commissioners Justin Jones and April Becker cut the ribbon Thu Nov 19. Reports say the tunnels had already been open for a few weeks.
- **Confirmed facts:**
  - **Clark County construction notice (PRIMARY, read):**
    - Project: "CC-215 Western Beltway Trail Improvements, Fort Apache Rd to Peace Way and Charleston Blvd Undercrossing."
    - Contractor: Las Vegas Paving.
    - Contract: $18,665,000.
    - Construction started Nov 3, 2025. Estimated completion was August 2026.
  - **KLAS / 8 News Now, Nov 18 (read):**
    - Reported cost is $18.7M, funded by the RTC.
    - It describes the tunnels and the 1,635-ft extension.
    - The ribbon cutting was set for 8 a.m. Thursday at the NW corner of W. Charleston and the beltway.
  - **LVSportsBiz, Nov 19 (read), and Newzino citing County Public Works (read):**
    - The ribbon cutting happened.
    - "The undercrossings had already been open for a few weeks."
    - The tunnel has a few turns and a short path back up to the trail at grade.
